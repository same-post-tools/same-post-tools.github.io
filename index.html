SAME Post Tools

Free, unofficial browser tools for SAME Post leaders and members: find events, track Post membership, build newsletter pieces, and turn routine exports into reports.

Your files stay on your computer. Every tool here runs entirely in your web browser. Files you open in a tool are read and processed on your own device and are never uploaded, sent, or saved anywhere else. There is no account, no sign-in, and no tracking.

You can check this yourself: each page blocks outside connections, and the code is public. Try a tool with your internet turned off; it still works.

About

SAME Post Tools grew out of the Industry Days list that Bobbi Jo Lang builds by hand, on her own time, every week, so Posts can plan their events around each other and members can plan their attendance. Her list is the foundation of this site.

The tools grew from an idea of Scott DeGaro's: group events that fall in the same week and region into one trip, first tried as a Gulf Coast trip in December 2026. Scott, a member of the SAME Membership Community of Interest, built them with the help of AI tools.
