SAME Post Member Roster Report

Open your Post's member roster export from SAME National and see who your members are, whose renewals are coming due, and who joined or lapsed since an earlier export.

Your roster stays on this computer. The files you open are read here and are never uploaded, sent, or saved. Nothing is kept after you close the page.

Contact details are never kept. Email addresses, phone numbers, and street addresses are dropped the moment a file is read. A ZIP code is turned into an approximate area for the member map and then dropped. This page never shows or exports any of them. Your original files are unchanged.

      What this page reads

      SAME National's member and organization exports for a Post, as .csv files, available to Post officers. The page recognizes each file by its column headings: a file with a SAME Id column is a member file; a file with a Company Name column and no SAME Id is an organization file. Current and grace-period files are combined.

      It keeps only these columns: names (to show lists on screen), SAME Id (to match members between exports), Member Type, Young Professional, SAME Fellow, Service Branch, veteran, Company Connection, Primary Occupation, Member Status, join dates, Member Thru, and mailing state and country; for organizations, Company Name, Member Type, small business status and categories, Member Status, join dates, and Member Thru. Every other column, including all contact details, is dropped as the file is read.

      The member map

      Each member is placed by the ZIP code of their mailing address, which is turned into an approximate location and a U.S. Census metro or micro area as the file is read; the ZIP code itself is not kept. A mailing address is the member's choice and is often an office. Groups: members within about an hour's drive of your Post; then each metro or micro area with 5 or more members; then each state with 5 or more, leaving out members already in a metro group (shown as "Rest of" the state); everyone else is under Elsewhere. Each member is counted once. Groups smaller than 5 are combined into Elsewhere so no small group points to one person. Connecticut addresses are grouped by state (Census changed Connecticut's counties in 2023). ZIP code locations and areas: U.S. Census Bureau, public domain.

      What can leave the page

      • Counts (the counts CSV and the printed summary): no names and no SAME IDs.
      • Named lists (new, lapsed, renewals due, and any count you open): on screen, and as an internal file or printout only after you confirm. Each is marked "Internal to Post officers" and has name, organization, member type, Member Thru, and status only.
      • Welcome new members newsletter block: names and organizations only. Check your own Post's privacy rules before you publish it.
      • Never: email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, ZIP codes. If you need more, your original export has it.

      Things to know

      • Years in SAME uses First Join Date. National's Chapter Join Date is often the member's organization's date, not the person's.
      • An individual membership belongs to the person. A member who changed employers keeps their membership and may still show the old company.
      • "Staff on the Post" for an organization counts members whose Company Connection matches the organization's name; names that are written differently may not match.
      • Lapsed means in the earlier export and not in the current one; it can also mean the member moved to another Post.
